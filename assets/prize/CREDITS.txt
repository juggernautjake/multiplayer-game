Everything else in the fiesta (the abuela and her chancla, the ring, Smiguel's big cartoon eyes, the piñata and its
candy, the lucha libre mask, the giant sombrero, the tortilla, the fireworks, the lights and the screen effects) is
drawn by the game itself, and its sounds (air horn, "ay ay ay" siren, whooshes, the piñata's whacks and burst, the
slide whistle, the boing, the ring's ting, fireworks) are made in scripts/synth.gd.

"My precious" (precious.wav / precious_custom.wav): no film or TV audio is used. precious.wav (added 2026-11-06,
Jacob's pick of three takes, "A raspy") is an original generated line: Windows' built-in speech voice (System.Speech,
Microsoft David) saying "My precious. My preciousssss.", pitched up, with vibrato, tremolo, a soft overdrive and a
pink-noise hiss tail added with ffmpeg. A precious_custom.wav (or .ogg) here,
or in the prize folder next to the game's exe, replaces it; with neither, that beat is silent.
